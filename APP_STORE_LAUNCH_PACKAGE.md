## 📝 2. Full App Store Description

```text
Your body doesn’t need another confusing graph in the morning. It needs a clear decision.

Recovery Desk turns your daily recovery signals into an actionable answer before your workout begins:
🟢 PUSH — Your body is well-rested and primed for high-intensity output.
🟠 MODERATE — Balanced readiness. Steady-state work is ideal, but don't chase maxes.
🔴 RECOVER — Elevated fatigue detected. Prioritize rest, mobility, and recovery.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHY RECOVERY DESK IS DIFFERENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. CALIBRATED TO YOUR PERSONAL BASELINE
Most apps grade you against a generic stranger or population average. A "good" HRV or resting heart rate depends entirely on YOUR body. Recovery Desk learns your 14-day rolling baseline so your score reflects what is normal for you.

2. FOUR SIGNALS. ZERO NOISE.
We filter out the 20 unnecessary vanity metrics and focus strictly on the four vital markers that determine physical recovery:
• Heart Rate Variability (HRV) — Autonomic readiness
• Resting Heart Rate (RHR) — Cardiovascular rest state
• Sleep Duration — Energy & recovery window
• Activity Strain — Yesterday's physical load

3. TODAY'S ACTIONABLE PLAN
Instead of leaving you guessing what a score of "78" means, Recovery Desk provides a clear, tri-pillar plan every morning:
🏃 Training — Recommended workout intensity
🚶 Movement — Daily step and activity volume
😴 Sleep — Tonight's rest and wind-down target

4. COMPLETE TRANSPARENCY (NO BLACK BOX)
Never wonder why you received a score. Tap into our transparent calculation breakdown to see the exact points contributed by each signal, plus what helped and what held you back.

5. BUILT AROUND YOUR GOALS
Whether your focus is Training & Performance (Athletic), Daily Fitness (Consistency), or General Recovery (Balance), your daily guidance automatically adapts to what you are trying to accomplish.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SAFE WELLNESS NOTICE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Recovery Desk is an athletic decision-support and wellness tool designed to help you balance training strain and recovery. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any medical condition. Always listen to your body and consult a healthcare professional for clinical advice.
```

## 🎬 4. Three 15-Second Short-Form Video Scripts

### **Video 1: The "I Just Wanted an Answer" Hook**
* **Target Audience**: Everyday gym-goers and runners tired of complex tracker graphs.
* **Visual**:
  - *0–3s*: Person staring at a phone looking confused by a screen full of 20 graphs.
  - *Text on Screen*: *"My fitness tracker gives me 20 numbers... I just wanted to know if I can lift heavy today."*
  - *4–10s*: Cut to phone opening Recovery Desk: clean dawn theme, big **84 / MODERATE** score.
  - *11–15s*: Tap on Today's Plan: *"Training: Steady intensity aerobic or technique work. Avoid max-effort PRs today."*
  - *Voiceover / Outro*: *"Stop guessing your workouts. Recovery Desk gives you the decision."*

### **Video 2: The "Personal Baseline" Differentiation Hook**
* **Target Audience**: People who get discouraged by generic wearable comparisons.
* **Visual**:
  - *0–3s*: Fast text: *"Why you shouldn't compare your HRV to a stranger online."*
  - *4–8s*: Show graphic: Athlete A has normal HRV of 90 ms. Athlete B has normal HRV of 45 ms.
  - *9–12s*: Cut to Recovery Desk: Highlight *"YOUR 14-DAY BASELINE"*.
  - *13–15s*: *"Recovery Desk compares your body strictly against YOUR recent normal. Know when to push."*

### **Video 3: The "No Black Box" Transparency Demo**
* **Target Audience**: Analytical athletes who hate unexplained black-box scores.
* **Visual**:
  - *0–4s*: *"Why did my recovery app tell me to take it easy today?"*
  - *5–10s*: Open Recovery Desk Why screen:
    - Tap **What Held Back ↓**: HRV was 15% below baseline.
    - Tap **Calculation Details**: Points breakdown adding up to 58 / 100.
  - *11–15s*: *"Every point explained. No mystery formulas. Know when to push, know when to recover."*
